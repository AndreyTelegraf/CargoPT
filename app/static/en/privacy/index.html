Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the site and submit a request through CargoPT.

14 July 2026

1. Controller and contact

The controller is the operator of the CargoPT service. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact privacy@cargopt.pt. Complete legal identification will be published when formalisation of the activity is complete.

2. Data processed

We may process name, contacts, pickup and delivery, date, item description, access, floors, elevators, team or equipment needs, comments, technical security data and the general campaign source in the URL.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Receiving, organising and forwarding the request: pre-contractual steps and performance of the requested service.
  • Enabling contact, offers and tracking: performance of the requested service.
  • Preventing abuse and protecting the system: legitimate security and operational interests.
  • Meeting legal duties: legal obligation.

4. Sharing

Required data may be shared with suitable independent carriers, technical providers processing data for us and authorities where required by law.

5. Retention

Data is kept as needed to manage the request, provide support, protect the service, meet legal duties and establish or defend claims. Criteria include request status, complaints, statutory periods and security needs.

6. Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection where applicable by contacting privacy@cargopt.pt. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority.

7. Security and transfers

We apply measures proportionate to risk. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, legally required safeguards will be used.

8. Device storage

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. “My requests” may store only technical identifiers and request links on the device. See the Cookie Policy.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated for legal, technical or operational changes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about moving services in vila nova de gaia

Is it enough to just indicate that the move is in Vila Nova de Gaia?

No. Please indicate the specific area or street when collecting and delivering, as access may vary.

What details should I confirm about the property?

Check floors, stairs, doors, hallways, lifts, gates and garage access.

Does the distance to the vehicle influence the service?

Yes. A long, inclined route or with steps can increase the time and number of helpers needed.

When should I send photos?

Send photographs of large furniture, special items and access points that are difficult to describe.

How to compare different quotes?

Confirm that they all consider the same load, the same access, helpers and additional services.

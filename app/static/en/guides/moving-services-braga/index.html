What to confirm before moving to Braga

Indicate the specific collection and delivery area, the date, inventory, floors, lifts, stairs and the point where the vehicle can stop. Also inform the manual carrying distance and whether you need helpers, disassembly, assembly or packing. How to prepare inventory for moving in Braga

Organize the load by rooms or categories and separate furniture, appliances, boxes and special items.

Indicate approximate measurements and send photographs when there are doubts about doors, stairs or lifts.

Furniture

List beds, mattresses, sofas, tables, cabinets, bookshelves and other large furniture.

Household Appliances

Identify refrigerator, machines, television and other relevant equipment.

Boxes

Indicate an approximate quantity and highlight fragile or especially heavy boxes.

Special items

Mark heavy, fragile, valuable or difficult-to-move items.

Services that must be defined

The quote must indicate the vehicle, the number of helpers, loading, unloading and protection of items.

Disassembly, assembly, packing, materials and additional equipment must be agreed in advance.

Helpers

Confirm how many people will participate in loading and unloading.

Disassembly and assembly

Identify the furniture covered and who will perform each task.

Packing and protection

Confirm the materials planned for furniture, appliances and fragile items.

Additional equipment

Inform about heavy parts or access that may require specific means.

How to compare quotes for a move in Braga

Send the same description to all carriers, including location, inventory, access and tasks.

Compare the price together with the vehicle, the team, the services included and the conditions presented.

  • The origin and destination are the same.
  • The inventory considered is identical.
  • Access and parking were described.
  • The number of helpers is indicated.
  • Additional services are detailed.
  • The price and conditions are recorded in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about moving services in braga

Is it enough to just indicate that the move is in Braga?

No. Please indicate the specific area or street when collecting and delivering, as access may vary.

What details should I confirm about the property?

Check floors, stairs, doors, hallways, lifts, gates and garage access.

Does the distance to the vehicle influence the service?

Yes. A long, inclined route or with steps can increase the time and number of helpers needed.

When should I send photos?

Send photographs of large furniture, special items and access points that are difficult to describe.

How to compare different quotes?

Confirm that they all consider the same load, the same access, helpers and additional services.

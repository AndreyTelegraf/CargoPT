Washing machine transport

How to transport a washing machine

The machine must remain empty, drained, protected and with the drum immobilized in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT

Direct answer

Drain the water and protect the drum before transport

To transport a washing machine, turn off electricity and water, completely drain the equipment and secure hoses and cable. Install the screws or transport systems indicated by the manufacturer to immobilize the drum. Keep the machine upright, protect surfaces and secure it to the vehicle.

Key points

Essential steps

  • Turn off electricity, water inlet and sewage.
  • Drain residual water before moving the machine.
  • Immobilize the drum according to the manufacturer's manual.
  • Transport the machine upright and well protected.
  • Save transport systems for future use.

Turn off the washing machine correctly

Remove the plug from the socket, close the supply tap and disconnect the inlet and discharge hoses. Have containers and cloths prepared to collect residual water.

Do not pull the machine by the cable or hoses. Confirm that all connections are clear before moving the equipment away.

Remove residual water

Even after a completed cycle, water may remain in the pump, filter and hoses. Consult the manual to safely drain and clean the filter.

After emptying, keep hoses elevated or protected to prevent further spills.

  • Close the water supply.
  • Disconnect the hoses.
  • Drain the pump and filter.
  • Dry the detergent drawer.
  • Attach hoses and cable to the equipment.

Immobilize the drum

Many machines use screws or transport elements to prevent excessive movement of the drum. The absence of these components may increase the risk of internal damage.

Consult the manual to identify the correct parts and installation method. Do not improvise systems that press or puncture machine components.

Prepare access

Measure the machine, doors, hallways, elevators and stairs. Remove obstacles and protect the floor before moving.

The machine is compact, but heavy and difficult to hold. Use a suitable cart and sufficient helpers for access.

Ports

Confirm the usable width and consider hinges, handles and baseboards.

Stairs

Indicate the number of flights, curves, width and condition of the steps.

Elevator

Check internal dimensions, maximum load and route to the entrance.

Parking

Enter the distance between the property and the place where the vehicle can stop.

How to transport in the vehicle

Keep the machine upright. Protect panels, doors, controls and corners with blankets or suitable materials.

Secure the equipment to safe points in the vehicle to prevent movement. Do not place heavy items on the machine.

Care before using again

At destination, remove the shipping bolts before using the machine. Save them for a future move.

Confirm the leveling, the condition of the hoses and the absence of leaks. The electrical and hydraulic installation must comply with the manufacturer's manual.

Frequently asked questions

Washing Machine Transport Questions

Is it mandatory to use transport bolts?

Follow the manufacturer's manual. Many models require screws or special elements to immobilize the drum during transport.

Can I transport the machine lying down?

It is recommended to transport it vertically. Always confirm the manufacturer's specific instructions.

How to remove all the water?

Use the drainage procedure indicated in the manual, including pump, filter and hoses, and have containers prepared for waste water.

Can I start the machine immediately after delivery?

First remove the transport elements, level the machine, connect the hoses correctly and check that there are no leaks.

What information should I give the carrier?

Indicate dimensions, floors, elevators, stairs, parking, distance to the vehicle and whether it requires disconnection or installation.

Direct answer

What to confirm before moving to Évora

Indicate the specific collection and delivery locations, the date, inventory, floors, lifts, stairs and where the vehicle can stop. Also inform the manual carrying distance, the necessary services and the items that require special protection. In Évora, the protected historic center, the pavement, conditioned access and the differences between urban neighborhoods and housing influence the service.

Key points

Information needed to receive useful quotes

  • Identify specific collection and delivery locations.
  • Confirm where the vehicle can stop at both ends.
  • Describe floors, lifts, stairs, gates, and garages.
  • Send photographs of the largest items and difficult access.
  • Compare quotes based on the same load and the same tasks.

Areas and access conditions in Évora

The generic indication of “Évora” may not be enough. The specific area influences the vehicle's approach and manual route.

Indicate the location at both ends and separately describe the collection and delivery access points.

Historic center

Confirm restricted access, narrow streets, pavement, parking and manual route.

Horta das Figueiras

Indicate floors, lifts, garages and access conditions to buildings.

Malagueira

Describe the correct entrance, residential streets, parking and distance to the property.

Bacelo and Lady of Health

Confirm access to houses, gates, patios and space to maneuver the vehicle.

How to prepare inventory

Organize the load by rooms or categories and separate furniture, appliances, boxes and special items.

Indicate approximate measurements and send photographs when there are doubts about doors, stairs, lifts or protection.

Furniture

List sofas, beds, tables, cabinets, bookshelves and other large furniture.

Household Appliances

Identify refrigerators, machines, televisions and other equipment.

Boxes

Indicate an approximate quantity and highlight fragile or heavy boxes.

Special items

Mark fragile, valuable, heavy or difficult-to-move items.

Access, parking and route

When the vehicle cannot approach the entrance, indicate the distance, slope, steps and type of flooring.

  • Floor in collection and delivery.
  • Existence and approximate dimensions of lifts.
  • Stairs, doors, corridors and tight turns.
  • Possible place to stop the vehicle.
  • Distance between the vehicle and each entrance.
  • Gates, garages or access control.
  • Rules of condominiums or buildings.
  • Allowed times for loading and unloading.

Services that must be defined

The quote must indicate the vehicle, the number of helpers, loading, unloading and protection of items.

Disassembly, assembly, packing, materials and additional equipment must be agreed in advance.

Helpers

Confirm how many people will participate in loading and unloading.

Disassembly and assembly

Identify the furniture covered and who will perform each task.

Packing and protection

Confirm the materials planned for furniture, appliances and fragile items.

Additional equipment

Inform about heavy parts or access that may require specific means.

How to compare quotes for change in Évora

Send the same description to all carriers, including locations, inventory, access and tasks.

Compare the price together with the vehicle, the team, the protection, the services included and the conditions presented.

  • The origin and destination are the same.
  • The inventory considered is identical.
  • The access conditions to both locations were described.
  • The number of helpers is indicated.
  • Additional services are detailed.
  • The price and conditions are recorded in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about moving services in évora

What locations should I indicate?

Indicate the street or specific area of collection and delivery, not just the municipality or region.

What details should I confirm about the properties?

Check floors, stairs, doors, corridors, lifts, gates, garages and distance to the vehicle.

Does the distance to the vehicle influence the service?

Yes. A long, inclined route or with steps can increase the time and number of helpers needed.

When should I send photos?

Send photographs of large furniture, special items and access points that are difficult to describe.

How to compare different quotes?

Confirm that they all consider the same load, the same access, helpers, protection and additional services.

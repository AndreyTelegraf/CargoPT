Moving checklist

Complete moving checklist

A checklist reduces forgetfulness and allows you to distribute tasks into stages, instead of concentrating all the preparation in the last few days.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT

Direct answer

Break down the move into preparation, hiring, packing and installation

A complete moving checklist should cover the inventory, dates, property access, carrier booking, packing materials, dismantling, box labels, documents, essentials and a final check of both properties. Assign an owner and deadline to each task so you can track progress.

Key points

What can't be missed

  • Set the date and confirm access to both properties.
  • Create inventory before asking for quotes.
  • Book the carrier and confirm the service in writing.
  • Pack and label items by room.
  • Make a final check before handing over the keys.

Initial planning

Start by defining the date, locations and approximate volume. Separate what will be transported from what will be sold, donated or disposed of.

Record all tasks in a single document and assign a date to each one.

  • Confirm the possible moving date.
  • Check access to the new property.
  • Create inventory by room.
  • Separate goods that will not be transported.
  • Identify heavy, fragile or valuable items.
  • Measure tight furniture and access.

Choose and confirm the carrier

Submit a complete request and compare equivalent quotes. Confirm the vehicle, team, tasks, date and price.

Keep in writing the chosen quote and the necessary contact details for the day of the move.

  • Send inventory and photographs.
  • Inform floors, elevators and stairs.
  • Confirm number of helpers.
  • Confirm packaging, disassembly and assembly.
  • Record date, time and payment conditions.
  • Confirm liability for damages.

Prepare materials and packaging

Gather boxes, tape, labels, and protective materials before you begin. Pack the least used items first.

Do not mix fragile items with heavy items and avoid boxes that are difficult to lift.

  • Boxes of various sizes.
  • Ribbon and labels.
  • Protection for fragile items.
  • Hardware bags.
  • Marker to identify boxes.
  • Protection of mattresses and furniture.

In the previous week

Confirm that most of the packaging is complete and review the inventory sent to the carrier.

Prepare documents, medicines, keys, chargers and essential items to go with you.

  • Confirm time with the carrier.
  • Update inventory changes.
  • Defrost and prepare appliances.
  • Separate documents and valuables.
  • Prepare a suitcase or box for essentials.
  • Reserve elevator or access when applicable.

On moving day

Keep corridors clear and indicate items that require greater care. Confirm inventory as you load.

Before leaving, check all rooms, closets, storage rooms, balconies and common areas.

  • Protect floors and passage areas.
  • Ensure access to keys.
  • Keep children and animals away from movement.
  • Confirm fragile and priority items.
  • Check meters and installations when necessary.
  • Carry out a final inspection of the property.

After arrival

Confirm that packages have reached their destination and place essential furniture first.

Check for visible damage and report any problems immediately. Do not turn on household appliances before following the precautions applicable to the model.

  • Check boxes and main items.
  • Check for visible damage.
  • Assemble essential beds and furniture.
  • Confirm water and electricity connections.
  • Save service documents.
  • Update the address for the necessary services.

Frequently asked questions

Moving Checklist Questions

When should I start the checklist?

As soon as you have a probable date. You can add details as you confirm inventory and services.

Do I need to list all items?

List the main furniture, appliances, special items and approximate number of boxes.

What should follow with me?

Documents, medicines, valuables, keys, essential equipment and essential items.

How to avoid forgetting items?

Carry out the final check by room, including closets, storage rooms, balconies and common spaces.

Should I save the carrier's quote?

Yes. Keep the service, value, team, tasks and agreed conditions in writing.

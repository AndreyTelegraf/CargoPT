How to order piano transport in Lisbon

To request piano transport in Lisbon, indicate whether it is upright, grand or another type, provide dimensions, weight when known and complete photographs. Describe the collection and delivery locations, floors, elevators, stairs, landings, parking and distance to the vehicle. Confirm whether partial disassembly will be necessary and who will perform it. The quote must identify the team's experience, number of helpers, equipment, protection, attachment to the vehicle, placement at the destination and conditions of responsibility. Only compare answers based on the same instrument, route, and task set.

Preparation before collection

Identify the type of piano, dimensions and weight when known. Photograph the instrument and confirm whether any partial disassembly will be necessary.

Inform in advance what will already be prepared and what should be carried out by the team.

Do not remove structural components without knowing the procedure. Close and protect the lid and keyboard, remove only expected loose parts and report any existing damage. If disassembly of legs, pedals, or other parts is necessary, confirm who will do it, how the parts will be identified, and whether reassembly is included.

Confirm experience, number of helpers, carts, straps, blankets, ramps or other means provided. For complex stairs or access, ask for a sufficiently detailed assessment before accepting the quote.

Protection and transportation in the vehicle

Keyboard, cover, pedals, corners and finish must be protected. Also confirm the conditions of responsibility.

Confirm how the item will be protected and immobilized to avoid impacts and displacements during the journey.

Keyboard, trim, corners, pedals and disassembled components need individual protection. The instrument must be stable and properly secured in the vehicle, without contact with volumes that could exert pressure. At destination, confirm the division and final position to avoid unnecessary movement after unloading.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about piano transport in Lisbon

What photos should I send?

Ship the instrument front, sides and back, including keyboard, cover, pedals, feet and special components. Photograph the entire route, with doors, elevators, stairs, landings and parking.

How many people are needed?

It depends on the type, weight, dimensions and access. A grand piano, walk-up floors or tight curves may require a larger team, partial disassembly or specific equipment.

Is preparation included in transportation?

Not automatically. Confirm that the team will carry out partial disassembly, protection, parts identification and reassembly. On a grand piano, these tasks must be evaluated by appropriate professionals.

How to avoid damage during the journey?

Use full protection, experienced staff, compatible equipment and stable attachment to the vehicle. The instrument must not be dragged or receive pressure from other volumes during travel.

What can change the price of the quote?

The actual type and weight, stairs, distant parking, need for disassembly, more helpers, additional equipment, schedule changes or a final placement different from that described may change the price.

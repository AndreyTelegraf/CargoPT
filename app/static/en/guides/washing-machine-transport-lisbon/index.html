Washing machine transport in Lisbon

Transporting a washing machine in Lisbon requires complete drainage, immobilization of the drum according to the manufacturer, verification of access, sufficient equipment and vertical fixing in the vehicle.

How to order washing machine transport in Lisbon

To request transportation of a washing machine in Lisbon, indicate the type of appliance, dimensions, photographs and collection and delivery locations. Confirm that it is disconnected, drained, dry and prepared with the elements recommended by the manufacturer to immobilize the drum. Describe floors, elevators, stairs, doors, parking and distance to the vehicle. Inform who will do the disconnection, preparation, loading, unloading and placement at the destination. Compare quotes that identify helpers, equipment, protection, transport position and applicable conditions if access or tasks are different.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about washing machine transport in Lisbon

What photos should I send?

Ship the machine front, sides and rear, including connections, hoses and installation space. Also photograph doors, corridors, elevators, stairs, landings and parking lots.

How many people are needed?

It depends on the weight, dimensions and access. Stairs, floors without elevators, narrow corridors or long distances to the vehicle may require more help.

Is preparation included in transportation?

Not automatically. Confirm who will turn off water and power, do drainage, place transportation elements, and secure hoses and cable before collection.

How to avoid damage during the journey?

Prepare the drum according to the manufacturer, protect the door, controls, hoses and surfaces and keep the machine stable in the vehicle. Avoid transporting it with residual water.

What can change the price of the quote?

Lack of preparation, floors without elevator, distant parking, more helpers, removal from a built-in position, schedule changes or requests for a connection at the destination may change the price.

Saving on a move

How to reduce the cost of a move

Reducing the cost does not mean choosing only the lowest price. The aim is to reduce the work required without omitting important service conditions.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT

Direct answer

Save by reducing volume, time and additional tasks

To reduce the cost of a move, remove items you no longer want to transport, prepare boxes and small items in advance, dismantle only furniture you can safely reassemble, make access easier and provide a complete inventory. Compare offers with the same scope and confirm everything in writing.

Key points

Key points

  • Move only the items you intend to keep.
  • Prepare boxes and small items before the team arrives.
  • Make parking and property access easier.
  • Avoid last-minute changes to the inventory.
  • Compare tasks, team and conditions as well as price.

Reduce the volume before requesting offers

Items you no longer need increase the space required and the time spent loading and unloading.

Separate in advance everything that will be sold, donated, recycled or discarded.

Prepare boxes and small items

Packed, closed and labelled boxes are easier to handle.

Avoid boxes that are excessively heavy or do not provide adequate protection.

  • Use sturdy boxes.
  • Distribute the weight between several boxes.
  • Protect fragile items.
  • Label the contents and destination room.
  • Close the boxes before the team arrives.

Consider dismantling furniture

Dismantling some furniture in advance may reduce working time, but only when you can do it without damaging the parts.

Keep screws, fittings and instructions organised.

Make access and parking easier

Clear corridors, available lifts and a nearby loading area reduce unnecessary carrying.

Report restrictions, stairs and distances to the vehicle in advance.

Compare equivalent offers

An offer is more economical only when it includes the work you actually need.

Check the number of movers, vehicle, materials, tasks and conditions that apply to changes.

Avoid savings that create additional costs

Omitting items, floors or access difficulties may require the service to be changed on moving day.

A complete description protects both the customer and the carrier.

Prepare a more efficient move

Reduce the volume, organise the items and describe the work accurately before comparing offers.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Is moving on a weekday always cheaper?

There is no universal rule. Availability and price depend on each carrier, date and service.

Can I dismantle all the furniture myself?

Only dismantle furniture you can safely take apart and reassemble without losing fittings or damaging the parts.

Do fewer movers mean a lower cost?

Not always. An insufficient team may increase the time required or make handling heavy items unsuitable.

Should I automatically choose the lowest offer?

No. First confirm that it includes the same vehicle, team, tasks, materials and conditions.

Can last-minute changes increase the price?

They can change the work required. Report any change before the date and confirm the new conditions.

What you should confirm before moving to Loures

To receive suitable quotes for a move in Loures, indicate the specific collection and delivery area, the date, inventory, floors, lifts, stairs and the place where the vehicle can stop. State the manual carrying distance and confirm whether you need helpers, disassembly, assembly, packing or additional protection. The municipality of Loures includes dense urban areas and more dispersed areas. The parish and specific location must always be included in the request.

How to compare quotes for a move in Loures

Send the same description to all carriers, including area, inventory, access, distance to the vehicle and necessary tasks.

Compare the price together with the vehicle, the team, the services included, the schedule and the conditions for changing the order.

  • The origin and destination are the same.
  • The inventory considered is identical.
  • Access and parking were described.
  • The number of helpers is indicated.
  • Additional services are detailed.
  • The price and conditions are recorded in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about moving services in loures

Is it enough to just indicate that the change is in Loures?

No. Indicate the specific area or street when collecting and delivering, as access and distance to the vehicle may vary.

What details should I confirm about the property?

Check floors, stairs, width of doors and corridors, curves, lifts, gates and access to the garage.

Does the distance to the vehicle influence the service?

Yes. A long, inclined or stepped route increases manual handling and may require more time or helpers.

When should I send photos?

Send photographs of large furniture, special items, stairs, narrow doors and other difficult access.

How to compare different quotes?

Confirm that they all consider the same load, the same access, the same number of helpers and the same services.

How to request furniture transport in Lisbon

To request furniture transport in Lisbon, prepare a list of the items and provide approximate dimensions for the largest pieces. Send photographs of sofas, beds, tables, wardrobes, shelving units and other furniture that may be difficult to move. Describe pickup and delivery access separately, including floors, lifts, stairs, doors, corridors, parking and the distance to the vehicle. Confirm whether you need transport only or also movers, dismantling, assembly, protection or placement at the destination. Compare offers calculated from the same information.

Key points

Information needed to receive comparable offers

  • List the furniture and send photographs of large items.
  • Provide dimensions and identify dismantlable pieces.
  • Describe floors, lifts, stairs, doors and corridors.
  • State where the vehicle can stop at each address.
  • Define movers, protection, dismantling and assembly.

Prepare a furniture inventory

Identify every item to be transported and separate large, heavy, fragile or difficult-to-handle pieces.

Include general photographs and mention modular furniture, glass doors, mechanisms, drawers and loose components.

Sofas and armchairs

State the number of seats, modules, chaise longue, sofa-bed mechanism and removable components.

Beds and mattresses

Provide dimensions and describe the frame, headboard, base and dismantling requirements.

Tables and chairs

Describe materials, removable tops, glass parts and the number of chairs.

Wardrobes and shelving units

Provide dimensions, doors, shelves, modules and dismantling conditions.

Measure the furniture and passageways

Record the width, height and depth of the largest items in the configuration in which they will be transported.

Compare those measurements with doors, corridors, lifts, stairs, landings and the space available to turn each piece.

  • Width, height and depth of the largest items.
  • Usable door openings.
  • Lift interior and door dimensions.
  • Stair width and turns.
  • Corridors and turning space.
  • Gates, entrances and the external route.

Describe access at both addresses

Pickup and delivery conditions should be described separately. Two nearby buildings may require very different teams and working times.

State floors, lifts, stairs, the distance between the entrance and the vehicle, and any condominium rules or time restrictions.

Lifts

Confirm dimensions, capacity, permitted hours and any reservation or protection requirements.

Stairs

State the number of flights, width, turns, landings and handrails.

Parking

Explain where the vehicle can stop and the distance to the entrance.

Internal route

Describe reception areas, garages, courtyards, ramps, steps and corridors.

Define how the furniture will be prepared

Remove stored items, loose shelves, drawers and components that could move during handling.

Furniture should only be dismantled by someone who knows the procedure. Include dismantling and reassembly in the request when the team should perform those tasks.

  • Empty wardrobes, chests and shelving units.
  • Remove or secure loose components.
  • Label screws, fittings and dismantled pieces.
  • Confirm who dismantles and who assembles.
  • Identify fragile parts and sensitive surfaces.
  • Define the required placement at the destination.

Confirm the team, equipment and protection

The number of movers should reflect the weight, furniture dimensions and access difficulty.

Confirm how corners, surfaces, glass, fabric, leather, floors, walls and lifts will be protected and how the furniture will be secured in the vehicle.

Movers

Confirm how many people will load, unload and carry items on stairs.

Equipment

Ask what equipment is planned for heavy items, long routes or restricted access.

Protection

Define materials for furniture, property surfaces and shared building areas.

Securing inside the vehicle

Furniture should remain stable and protected against impacts and movement.

How to compare offers

Send every carrier the same furniture list, photographs, measurements, addresses, access conditions and required tasks.

Compare the price together with the number of movers, vehicle, materials, dismantling, assembly, placement and conditions for changes.

  • The same inventory is being considered.
  • Both addresses and access conditions are described.
  • The number of movers is stated.
  • Dismantling and assembly are itemised.
  • Protection, loading and unloading are included.
  • Price, date and conditions are recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about furniture transport in Lisbon

What information should I provide about the furniture?

Send a list, photographs and approximate dimensions of the largest items. Identify fragile, heavy or modular pieces and furniture requiring dismantling.

How many movers are required?

It depends on weight, dimensions, stairs, lifts, the carrying distance and whether the furniture can be dismantled.

Are dismantling and assembly automatically included?

No. Confirm which pieces will be dismantled, who will perform the work, which materials will be used and whether assembly at the destination is included.

Can parking conditions affect the service?

Yes. When the vehicle cannot stop near the entrance, the manual carrying distance increases and more time or movers may be required.

Does CargoPT perform the transport?

No. CargoPT forwards the request to independent carriers. The selected professional performs the transport service.

How to choose a moving company

A good choice starts by comparing quotes based on the same inventory, access conditions and tasks.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT

Direct answer

Compare capacity, clarity and service conditions

To choose a moving company, send the same complete description to several movers and compare the vehicle, team, tasks, price and conditions. Check contacts, identification, relevant experience, liability for damages and what happens in case of changes. Avoid deciding just on the lowest price.

Key points

Comparison criteria

  • Compare quotes based on the same inventory.
  • Confirm vehicle, team and experience for the service.
  • Ask for a written description of the tasks included.
  • Check liability, payments and changes.
  • Be wary of vague answers or contradictory conditions.

Send the same request to every company

Send the same route, date, list of items, photographs, access points and tasks to all companies.

Without a common basis, different values may correspond to different services.

Confirm service capability

The vehicle, number of helpers and equipment must be suitable for the volume and access.

When there are pianos, heavy appliances, stairs or large furniture, ask about their experience in similar situations.

  • Vehicle type and capacity.
  • Number of helpers.
  • Handling equipment.
  • Experience with special items.
  • Capacity for disassembly and assembly.
  • Availability on the required date.

Ask for a clear written quote

The quote must indicate the service included, the price, date, locations and main conditions.

Confirm whether packaging, materials, disassembly, loading, unloading and assembly are part of the price or charged separately.

Check identity and contacts

Confirm the identification used in the quote, the available contacts and who will be responsible for the service.

Reviews and references can be helpful, but should be considered alongside the clarity of the quote and suitability for the job.

Confirm responsibility and conditions

Ask how damages, delays, cancellations and changes to inventory or access are handled.

Record agreements in writing and clarify in advance when and how payment will be made.

Damage

Confirm the registration and liability procedure.

Changes

Ask how additional items or tasks affect the service.

Cancellation

Check applicable deadlines and conditions.

Payment

Confirm agreed value, time and method.

Warning signs before choosing

Answers that ignore inventory and hits may not reflect actual work.

Be careful with conditions that change repeatedly, lack of identification, pressure to decide immediately or payment requests without a clear record.

  • Value presented without questions about the service.
  • Refusal to confirm tasks in writing.
  • Contradictory information about team or vehicle.
  • Unclear payment terms.
  • Lack of verifiable contacts.
  • Pressure to decide without clarifying doubts.

Compare companies with the same description

Indicate inventory, access conditions and tasks to receive quotes that are easier to compare.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about choosing moving companies

Should I choose the cheapest quote?

Not automatically. Confirm that it includes the same vehicle, team, tasks, materials and conditions.

Is it important to have a written quote?

Yes. Registration helps confirm the service, value, date and responsibilities.

How to evaluate experience with special items?

Ask for similar services, team, equipment and procedure planned for the item and access conditions.

Should I pay in advance?

Confirm in writing the amount, timing and terms of payment before accepting.

What changes could modify the quote?

Additional items, different access, new tasks, date changes or the need for more helpers may change the service.

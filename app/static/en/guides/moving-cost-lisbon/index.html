Moving costs in Lisbon

How much does a move cost in Lisbon?

The cost depends on the load, access conditions, distance, required team and included services. A complete description allows you to compare offers for the same work.

Published on 2026-07-24 · Reviewed by CargoPT

Direct answer

There is no single price for a move in Lisbon

The cost of a move in Lisbon varies according to the volume and type of items, pickup and delivery locations, floors, lifts, whether the vehicle can park near the properties, the number of movers and services such as dismantling, assembly or packing. To receive comparable offers, send every carrier the same description of the service.

Key points

Key points

  • The quantity and type of items determine the vehicle and team.
  • Floors, lifts and stairs change the work required.
  • The carrying distance between the vehicle and entrance must be stated.
  • Dismantling, assembly and packing should be specified.
  • Always compare the scope of the offer, not only the price.

What determines the cost of a move?

The carrier estimates the service from the load, access, route, number of people and expected working time.

An incomplete description may lead to an offer that does not cover all the work required.

Load

Furniture, appliances, boxes and special items affect the vehicle and duration.

Access

Upper floors without a lift, narrow stairs and long carrying routes increase manual work.

Team

The number of movers should match the weight, volume and conditions at the properties.

Services

Packing, dismantling, assembly and materials should be confirmed separately.

Why is parking information important?

Where the vehicle can stop affects the distance the team must carry the items.

State whether there is direct access, reserved parking or a longer carrying distance.

How to prepare the inventory

Organise the list by room and identify the main furniture and appliances.

Add an estimate of the number of boxes and photographs of large, heavy or fragile items.

  • Sofas, beds, mattresses, tables and wardrobes.
  • Refrigerator, machines, television and other equipment.
  • Approximate number of boxes.
  • Heavy, fragile or irregularly shaped items.
  • Furniture that needs dismantling.

How to compare offers for Lisbon

Confirm that every offer was calculated using the same inventory, route and access conditions.

Also check the number of movers, included tasks, materials and conditions for changes.

How to avoid unnecessary costs

Prepare boxes and small items in advance and remove anything you do not intend to move.

Make access easier, confirm parking and report changes before the service date.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a price without listing the items?

A generic description rarely allows the complete service to be estimated. Provide at least the main furniture, appliances and an estimate of the boxes.

Does an upper floor without a lift affect the offer?

Yes. Carrying items on stairs may require more time or more people and should be reported in advance.

Should parking be confirmed beforehand?

It is advisable to explain where the vehicle can stop and the distance to the property entrances.

Is furniture dismantling always included?

No. Confirm exactly which furniture will be dismantled or assembled and whether that work is included.

How should I compare different offers?

Check that they include the same inventory, access conditions, team, materials and additional services.

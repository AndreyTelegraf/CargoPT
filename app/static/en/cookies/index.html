Technology and privacy

Cookie Policy

CargoPT uses the Meta Pixel only after the user allows optional advertising cookies.

25 August 2026

1. Necessary and optional cookies

The site works without accepting advertising. Necessary storage keeps privacy choices and, after a request is submitted, shortcuts for “My requests”. Meta cookies are optional and remain blocked until consent is given.

2. Meta Pixel

If you allow it, we load the Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, to measure advertising effectiveness. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

3. Events and data

We send only the PageView and Lead events. We do not send name, phone, WhatsApp, email, addresses, cargo description, comments or the private request link. Meta may receive the page URL, IP address and technical browser or device identifiers.

4. Choice and duration

The choice is stored in the browser for six months. You can reject without losing functionality or change the decision at any time in Cookie preferences.

5. Functional storage

After a request is submitted, the browser may store up to 20 technical records for “My requests”. Each contains only the ID, private tracking link and technical token.

6. How to delete

You can delete site data in browser settings. This removes choices and local shortcuts but does not cancel submitted requests.

7. Logs and external services

The server may create technical security and operation logs. External links may apply the external service’s own policies. See the Privacy Policy.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated if the relevant technologies, purposes or retention periods change.

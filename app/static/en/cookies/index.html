Technology and privacy

Cookie Policy

CargoPT works without analytics, advertising or profiling cookies and does not display a banner for optional technologies.

14 July 2026

1. Cookies

The public site does not use cookies for advertising, audience measurement, profiling or recognition across different sites.

2. Functional storage

After a request is submitted, the browser may store up to 20 technical records for “My requests”. Each contains only the request ID, private tracking link and technical token.

3. What is not stored

Name, phone, WhatsApp, email, addresses, cargo description, comments and other form fields are not stored locally.

4. How to delete

You can remove this data in browser settings by deleting site data for cargopt.pt. This removes local shortcuts but does not cancel submitted requests.

5. Server logs

The server may create technical logs required for security and operation. These are not cookies and are processed under the Privacy Policy.

6. External services

External links, such as Telegram, may apply the external service’s own policies after you leave CargoPT.

7. Future changes

If optional technologies are introduced, they will not be activated before any required consent is obtained.

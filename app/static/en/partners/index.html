Criteria

What we look for

This page is not an open directory. Each organization must have a practical connection to the CargoPT user journey.

CargoPT's role: we organize requests and connect customers with independent transport providers. We do not operate our own fleet and do not guarantee partner prices, availability or outcomes.

Verifiable identity

The company, service, contact and public presence must be confirmable.

Concrete relevance

The collaboration should address a recognizable need for customers, businesses or transport professionals.

Transparent conditions

Payments, sponsorships, qualifications, exclusivity and data obligations must be disclosed before publication. Any commercial relationship will be identified.

Separate responsibility

Each organization remains responsible for its own services, terms, prices and data processing.

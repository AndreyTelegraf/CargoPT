FAQ

How much does transport cost?

The price depends on route, volume, floors, elevator access, date and whether movers are needed.

Do I pay to submit a request?

No. The request is free for clients.

Can I move one item only?

Yes. You can request transport for furniture, appliances, a piano, boxes or other single items.

Can I move between cities?

Yes. CargoPT works for local and intercity transport across Portugal.

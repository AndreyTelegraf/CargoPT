FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does transport cost?

The price depends on the distance, cargo volume, floors, elevator access, the chosen date and whether movers are needed. Each carrier sends their own offer, so you can compare prices and conditions before deciding.

Do I pay to submit a request?

No. The service is free for anyone looking for transport. You can describe the move, receive offers and compare options without paying for the request.

Can I move one item only?

Yes. You can request transport for furniture, appliances, a piano, boxes or other individual items. Just specify what needs to be moved, the locations, access conditions and preferred date.

Can I move between cities?

Yes. You can receive offers for local transport and moves between cities in Portugal, including Lisbon, Porto, Cascais, Sintra, Oeiras and other locations.
